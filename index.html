Cindy Pacing Strategy

1. Break The Pull-Ups Early Don't grind out 5 reps unbroken in Round 1. Start with 3 reps, take 2 deep breaths, then complete 2 reps. This prevents lat lactic acid lockup.
2. Strict Hollow-BodyMidline Keep feet slightly in front of the bar. A braced core transfers lat drive directly upward and eliminates sloppy swinging.
3. Air Squat Cadence Use the air squats as your aerobic recovery. Keep a rhythm of 1 squat per second (15 seconds total) before hopping back on the bar.
